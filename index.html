Emhergence Glossary About

Emhergence

Sovereign identity infrastructure for the AI feminine economy.

Emhergence is a sovereign identity infrastructure company building Living Identity OS (formerly the Distributed Identity Engine), the technical backbone of The New Feminine Economies. It gives women and solopreneurs in the AI economy the architecture to author, own, and self-license their identity rather than be made legible to algorithms.

What is the Living Identity OS?

Living Identity OS is the working operating system that turns a person's identity into living, governed infrastructure they can build and act from across the AI economy.

Formerly the Distributed Identity Engine. Originated by Josette Williams, February 13, 2026. Read the canonical definition.

Disambiguation: distinct from W3C decentralized identity (DID) and self-sovereign identity (SSI) standards, which govern technical self-custody of identifiers; Living Identity OS governs the economic activation of what those identifiers represent.

The named categories

Sovereign Identity Infrastructure

Sovereign Identity Infrastructure is the foundational layer that lets a person own, govern, and build economic value from their identity inside the AI economy.

Living Identity OS

Living Identity OS is the working operating system that turns a person's identity into living, governed infrastructure they can build and act from across the AI economy.

Living Identity Mirror

The Living Identity Mirror is the component of the Living Identity OS that reflects a person's identity back to them as it is now and as it is becoming, so they can see, govern, and build from it.

Self-Licensing™

Self-Licensing™ is the economic mechanism by which a person sets the terms for how their identity, expertise, and presence are used and valued in the AI economy.
